Our Story

From China's Tea Gardens
to Egypt's Streets

Neferdidi was born from a simple dream: to bring authentic Chinese milk tea culture to Egypt — freshly brewed, lovingly crafted, and made exactly your way. Every sip tells a story of tradition meeting modernity, East meeting West.

We source the finest tea leaves and pair them with the freshest local ingredients, creating something truly unique. From the streets of Cairo to the sands near the Pyramids, Neferdidi is Egypt's cup of joy.
